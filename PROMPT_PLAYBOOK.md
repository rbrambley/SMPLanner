# Suno Album + Social Rollout — AI Prompt Playbook

Use these prompts one at a time in a web-based AI like ChatGPT, Meta.ai, or Claude. Each block is designed to be copied and pasted as a single message. Answer the AI's questions, then paste the next prompt when you're ready.

---

## Prompt 1 — Create the album plan

```text
You are an expert Suno album producer, songwriter, and social media content strategist. You are producing a full album from start to finish.

Ask me the following questions one at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next question.

1. What is the album concept or story? (Describe the world, the main character, the emotion, or the central idea.)
2. What genre should the album be in? (e.g. rap, pop, rock, country, synthwave, trap, R&B, etc.)
3. What is the mood profile? (Give 3 to 5 moods that describe the album overall.)
4. How many tracks? (Recommend a number if I am unsure.)
5. What is the target total runtime? (e.g. 28-40 minutes.)
6. What are the theme words or imagery I want in song titles? (e.g. golf terms, city lights, street names, seasons, objects.)
7. Who are 1 to 3 reference artists or songs for the sound?
8. Should this album avoid any specific topics, names, or copyrighted brands?

After I answer all questions, create a full album plan and output it in this exact order:

- Album title
- Slug / folder name
- Album genre and style notes
- Mood profile
- Total runtime and average track length
- Track list table with these columns: #, working title, role (opener/single/ballad/wildcard/closer), mood, BPM, duration, and one-sentence theme
- A short "Album story arc" paragraph that explains how the track list flows
- A list of which tracks should get individual stems (opener, singles, closer + up to 3 others)
- 3 to 5 recommended song tags / keywords for YouTube and Suno

Do not use emojis in your response. Keep the titles unique and on-theme. Do not include any copyrighted brand names in titles unless I explicitly asked for them.
```

- IG/FB captions: 1 to 2 lines, end with a question, no emojis, no links in the caption itself (link goes in first comment)
- Instagram hashtags: 3 to 5 specific tags, bottom of caption

Output:
- A weekly posting calendar for 4 weeks (which track/day/time/platform)
