## Content Strategy

- **Primary discovery engine:** YouTube Shorts
- **Secondary:** Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels
- **Cross-post the best Shorts to Reels** with platform-specific captions
- **Turn top Shorts into full lyric videos** for monetization and search
- **Use Suno Hooks** to preview songs before making Shorts

## YouTube Shorts Format

1. **9:16 vertical**, 15–30 seconds
2. **Title card first 1–2 seconds** — big, bold text that stops the scroll
3. **One hook** — catchiest chorus or 4-line opener
4. **Simple background** — solid color, waveform, or lyric visualizer
5. **@vmetcone handle** visible the whole time
6. **Title/caption + hashtags** pasted from the calendar

## Caption and Hashtag Rules (Updated 8/21)

### Do not delete posts
- Editing a caption is better than deleting
- Archive duplicates instead of deleting
- Deleting can hurt the next few posts in the algorithm

### Links in captions
- **Do not put `suno.com`, `youtube.com`, or `linktr.ee` in IG/FB captions**
- Put the link in the **first comment** instead
- Example first comment: “Full lyric video: linktr.ee/vmetcone”

### Caption structure
- Keep it to 1–2 lines
- End with a question to spark comments
- Example: “Made this for Dacula. What street should be in verse 2?”

### Hashtags on Instagram
- Use **3–5 specific hashtags**, not 10–15
- Put them at the very bottom after a line break
- Good: `#dacula #daculaga #gwinnettcounty #vmone`
- Bad: `#rap #music #newmusic #suno` (too broad, get buried)

### Hashtags on Facebook
- **Avoid hashtags** on personal profile posts
- If you use one, use only your brand tag: `#vmone`
- Facebook suppresses posts that look like IG cross-posts
- Use the **location tag** instead of hashtags when relevant

### Location tags
- Instagram: use a specific location tag like “Dacula, Georgia” when the post is local
- Facebook: use the location tag; do not combine it with multiple hashtags

### Re-uploading
- Do not re-upload the exact same video file — it can be flagged as duplicate
- For the same song, cut a new 15-second 9:16 Reel with a different title card
- Keep full lyric videos as catalog; use 15-second Reels for discovery

### Posting times
- Aim for consistency: Tuesday/Thursday 7pm, Sunday 10am
- Same 3x/week pattern so the algorithm learns your schedule
